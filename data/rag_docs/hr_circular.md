# HR Circular HR/2026/014: Hyderabad Office Relocation

> **TRAINING DATA ONLY.** This document describes the fictional company
> *Lumen Ridge Technologies Pvt. Ltd.* It was written for the RAG intern lab and
> is **not** an actual company circular.

**Circular number:** HR/2026/014
**Date of issue:** 18 August 2026
**Applies to:** All employees and interns based at the Hyderabad office

## Summary

The Hyderabad office will relocate from the current Madhapur premises to a new
campus. Employees will report to the new campus from **Tuesday, 1 September
2026**.

## New Office Address

Lumen Ridge Technologies, **Tower B, Orion Tech Park, Gachibowli, Hyderabad**.
The Madhapur office will close permanently at the end of the working day on
**Wednesday, 26 August 2026**.

## Move Schedule

- **Thursday 27 August and Friday 28 August 2026:** All Hyderabad employees
  work from home while furniture and equipment are moved.
- **Monday 31 August 2026:** The facilities team completes setup; only the IT
  and Administration teams report to the new campus.
- **Tuesday 1 September 2026:** All Hyderabad employees report to the new
  campus.

## Employee Actions Before the Move

1. Pack personal items in the labelled crates provided by the Administration
   team by **Tuesday, 25 August 2026**.
2. Return any unused access cards for the Madhapur office to the reception
   desk.
3. Register for parking at the new campus (see below).

## Shuttle Service

A free shuttle will run between **Raidurg Metro Station** and the new campus
every **20 minutes** from **08:00 to 11:00** and from **16:30 to 20:30** on
working days.

## Parking

Parking at Orion Tech Park is allocated through the Administration team.
Employees must register their vehicle in the **FacilityDesk** portal by
**Tuesday, 25 August 2026**. Two-wheeler parking is free; four-wheeler parking
is allocated by lottery for September 2026 and reviewed monthly.

## Hybrid Anchor Days in September 2026

To reduce crowding while the new floors are commissioned, Hyderabad hybrid
employees will use **Wednesday and Friday** as anchor days for **September 2026
only**. From **1 October 2026** the default anchor days in the Remote and Hybrid
Work Policy apply again.

## Contacts

Questions about the relocation should be directed to the Hyderabad
Administration team at `hyd-admin@lumenridge.example`.
