## About This Calendar

holidays for **calendar year 2026** (1 January to 31 December 2026) at the
Hyderabad, Pune and Bengaluru offices. Every employee receives the common
holidays plus the holidays for their own base location, and may additionally
choose 2 optional holidays.

## Holiday Rules

### Weekend Holidays

When a holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, **no compensatory day off** is
given. In 2026 this applies to Independence Day (Saturday, 15 August).

### Holidays During Leave

Holidays that fall within an approved annual leave period are not counted as
leave days.

### Remote and Hybrid Employees

Remote and hybrid employees follow the holiday list of their **registered base
location**.

### Working on a Holiday

Employees asked to work on a declared holiday receive **one compensatory day
off**, which must be used within **30 days** of the holiday.
