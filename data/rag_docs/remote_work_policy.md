# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

> **TRAINING DATA ONLY.** This document describes the fictional company
> *Lumen Ridge Technologies Pvt. Ltd.* It was written for the RAG intern lab and
> is **not** an actual company policy.

## Purpose and Scope

This policy, owned by People Operations, sets out the rules for hybrid and fully
remote work. Version 2.0 takes effect on **1 July 2026** and replaces the
*Flexible Work Guidelines* issued in March 2024.

## Work Arrangements

Lumen Ridge recognises three work arrangements:

1. **Office-based** – the employee works from an office every working day.
2. **Hybrid** – the employee works from the office a minimum of **3 days per
   week** and remotely on the remaining days.
3. **Fully remote** – the employee works remotely and visits an office only for
   scheduled in-person events.

## Eligibility

### Hybrid Eligibility

An employee may opt for hybrid work if all of the following apply:

- the employee has **completed probation**;
- the employee's most recent performance rating is **"Meets Expectations" or
  higher**; and
- the employee's role is classified as **hybrid-eligible** by the department
  head.

### Fully Remote Eligibility

Fully remote work requires approval from both the **department head and People
Operations**. Fully remote employees must reside in **India**, and each fully
remote arrangement is reviewed **annually**.

## Anchor Days

Hybrid employees must be in the office on the anchor days for their location.

### Default Anchor Days

Unless a location rule below applies, the anchor days are **Tuesday and
Thursday**.

### Pune Office Anchor Days

Because of seat sharing at the Pune office, the anchor days for Pune-based
hybrid employees are **Monday and Wednesday**.

## Core Collaboration Hours

All employees, regardless of arrangement, must be available between **11:00 and
16:00 IST** on working days.

## Working From Abroad

Employees may work from outside India for a maximum of **20 working days per
calendar year**. Each request requires approval from **People Operations and
Legal** and must be submitted **at least 30 days** before the first day abroad.

## Equipment and Allowances

### Equipment

Every employee receives a company laptop. Monitors and headsets are available on
request through the IT service portal.

### Home Office Allowance

Fully remote employees receive a **one-time home office allowance of INR
15,000** in their first month on a fully remote arrangement.

### Internet Reimbursement

- Hybrid employees: **INR 1,000 per month**
- Fully remote employees: **INR 1,500 per month**

Internet reimbursement is claimed monthly through the **ExpensePro** portal with
a copy of the broadband bill.

## In-Person Events

Fully remote employees must attend the **quarterly planning meeting** at their
department's home office. Travel for these events is reimbursed under the
Travel and Expense Policy.

## Holidays for Remote Employees

Remote and hybrid employees follow the holiday list of their **registered base
location** as published in the company holiday calendar.

## Information Security

Company VPN must be used for all work performed outside an office. Public Wi-Fi
must never be used without the VPN, and confidential documents must not be
printed at home.
