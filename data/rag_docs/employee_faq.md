# Employee FAQ

> **TRAINING DATA ONLY.** This document describes the fictional company
> *Lumen Ridge Technologies Pvt. Ltd.* It was written for the RAG intern lab and
> is **not** actual company guidance.

This FAQ is maintained by People Operations and was last revised on 1 July 2026.
Where an answer summarises a policy, the policy document takes precedence.

## Joining and Probation

### How long is the probation period?

The probation period is **90 days** from the date of joining. Your manager
confirms your probation in PeopleHub at the end of this period.

### When will I receive my laptop?

Your company laptop is handed over on your first day by the IT team at your base
office. Fully remote joiners receive the laptop by courier before their start
date.

## Payroll and Benefits

### When is salary paid?

Salary is credited on the **last working day of each month**. Payslips are
available in PeopleHub on the same day.

### Who do I contact about payroll?

Send payroll questions to `payroll@lumenridge.example` or raise a PeopleHub
ticket in the *Payroll* category.

### What health insurance do I have?

Every full-time employee is covered by a **family floater health insurance
policy of INR 5 lakh**, covering the employee, spouse, up to two children and
up to two parents or parents-in-law.

### Is there an employee assistance programme?

Yes. The **CareCircle** programme provides up to **6 free confidential
counselling sessions** per year for employees and their dependants.

### Is there an employee referral bonus?

Yes. A referral bonus of **INR 50,000** is paid for engineering hires and
**INR 25,000** for all other roles. The bonus is paid after the referred
employee completes **90 days** with the company.

## Leave

### How many leave days can I carry forward?

You can carry forward up to **10 days** of unused annual leave into the next
leave year. Anything above 10 days lapses on 31 March. See the Leave Policy for
details.

### Where do I apply for leave?

All leave is applied for in **PeopleHub**.

## Remote Work

### Can I work from another country?

You may work from outside India for up to **20 working days per calendar
year**, with People Operations and Legal approval requested at least 30 days in
advance. See the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy.

### How do I claim internet reimbursement?

Submit a monthly claim in **ExpensePro** with your broadband bill attached. The
monthly amount depends on your work arrangement as set out in the Remote and
Hybrid Work Policy.

## Holidays

### Where can I find the holiday list?

The holiday calendar is published by the Administration team every January. It
lists the holidays common to all offices, location-specific holidays and the
optional holiday list.

### How many optional holidays can I take?

You may choose **2 optional holidays** per calendar year from the optional
holiday list, and must select them in PeopleHub at least 7 days in advance.

## Learning and Development

### Is there a learning budget?

Each employee has an annual learning budget of **INR 40,000** for courses,
certifications and conference tickets, approved by the reporting manager.
Unused budget does not carry forward to the next year.
