# Travel and Expense Policy

> **TRAINING DATA ONLY.** This document describes the fictional company
> *Lumen Ridge Technologies Pvt. Ltd.* It was written for the RAG intern lab and
> is **not** an actual company policy.

## Purpose and Scope

This policy, owned by the Finance department, governs business travel and the
reimbursement of business expenses for all employees and interns at every Lumen
Ridge office. Version 4.1 takes effect on **15 January 2026**.

## Travel Approval

### Domestic Travel

All domestic business travel must be pre-approved by the reporting manager in
the **TravelDesk** portal before any booking is made.

### International Travel

International travel requires approval from the **department head and the
CFO's office**. The request must be submitted in TravelDesk **at least 14
calendar days** before the planned departure date.

## Air Travel

Employees in bands A to C travel in **economy class**. Employees in band D and
above (directors and above) may book **premium economy** for flights longer than
**5 hours**. All flights are booked through TravelDesk; tickets booked outside
TravelDesk are reimbursed only with written justification.

## Accommodation

Hotel reimbursement is capped per night as follows:

| City category | Nightly cap |
| --- | --- |
| Tier-1 cities | INR 7,000 |
| Other domestic cities | INR 4,500 |
| International | USD 180 |

Tier-1 cities are Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Chennai.

## Per Diem

The daily allowance covers meals and incidental expenses:

- **Tier-1 cities:** INR 2,500 per day
- **Other domestic cities:** INR 1,800 per day
- **International travel:** USD 75 per day

Per diem is not paid for travel to the employee's own base location.

## Local Conveyance

Ride-hailing services and metered taxis used for business purposes are
reimbursed at actual cost. Use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at
**INR 12 per km** for a car and **INR 6 per km** for a two-wheeler.

## Receipts and Claim Submission

- An original receipt or invoice is required for every expense above
  **INR 500**.
- Expense claims must be submitted in the **ExpensePro** portal **within 30
  days** of the trip end date (or the expense date for non-travel expenses).
- Claims submitted **more than 60 days** after the trip are not reimbursed
  unless approved by the CFO's office.
- Approved claims are reimbursed **within 10 working days** of final approval.

## Client Entertainment

Client entertainment requires prior approval from the department head and is
capped at **INR 3,000 per attendee**. Alcohol is **not reimbursable** under any
category.

## Travel for Remote Employees

Fully remote employees who travel to an office for mandatory in-person events,
such as the quarterly planning meeting, are covered by this policy. Their
flights, accommodation and per diem follow the same limits as any other
business trip and must be approved in TravelDesk.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursed: alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic or
parking fines, laundry for trips shorter than 5 nights, and upgrades not
permitted by this policy.
