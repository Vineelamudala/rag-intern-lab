# Leave Policy

> **TRAINING DATA ONLY.** This document describes the fictional company
> *Lumen Ridge Technologies Pvt. Ltd.* It was written for the RAG intern lab and
> is **not** an actual company policy.

## Purpose and Scope

This policy defines the leave entitlements for employees of Lumen Ridge
Technologies at all office locations (Hyderabad, Pune and Bengaluru). It
applies to full-time employees, employees on probation, and interns. Contractors
engaged through third-party vendors are governed by their vendor agreements and
are not covered by this policy.

Version 3.0 of this policy takes effect on 1 April 2026 and replaces version 2.2
(effective 1 April 2024).

## Leave Year

The leave year runs from **1 April to 31 March**. Leave balances are calculated
and reset according to this leave year, not the calendar year.

## Annual Leave

### Entitlement

Full-time employees receive **18 days of annual leave** per leave year. Annual
leave accrues at **1.5 days per completed month** of service.

### Eligibility During Probation

The probation period is **90 days** from the date of joining. Employees accrue
annual leave during probation, but may avail a **maximum of 3 days** of annual
leave before probation is confirmed.

### Applying for Annual Leave

Annual leave is requested through the **PeopleHub** portal. Requests for more
than 3 consecutive days must be submitted **at least 7 calendar days in
advance**. The reporting manager must approve or reject a request within
**3 working days**.

### Carry Forward and Lapse

A maximum of **10 days** of unused annual leave may be carried forward into the
next leave year. Any balance above 10 days **lapses on 31 March**.

### Encashment

Annual leave cannot be encashed while in service. On separation, the
outstanding annual leave balance is encashed up to a **maximum of 30 days**,
calculated on basic salary.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive **10 days of sick leave** per leave year. Sick leave does not
carry forward and cannot be encashed. A medical certificate from a registered
medical practitioner is required for sick leave of **more than 2 consecutive
days**.

## Casual Leave

Employees receive **6 days of casual leave** per leave year. Casual leave may
not be taken for more than **3 consecutive days** and cannot be combined with
annual leave. Casual leave does not carry forward.

## Parental Leave

### Maternity Leave

Eligible employees receive **26 weeks of paid maternity leave** for each of the
first two children, and **12 weeks** for the third and subsequent children. To
be eligible, the employee must have worked for at least **80 days in the 12
months** preceding the expected date of delivery.

### Paternity Leave

Employees receive **10 working days of paid paternity leave**, which must be
taken within **6 months** of the birth or adoption of a child.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees receive **5 working days of paid bereavement leave** on the death of
an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law).

## Leave Without Pay

Leave without pay (LWP) of up to **30 days** may be granted after paid leave is
exhausted. LWP requires approval from both the reporting manager and the HR
Business Partner for the employee's department.

## Interns

Interns accrue **1 day of leave per completed month** of internship. Intern
leave does not carry forward, is not encashed at the end of the internship, and
interns are not eligible for parental leave under this policy.

## Holidays During Leave

Public holidays listed in the company holiday calendar that fall within an
approved annual leave period are **not deducted** from the annual leave balance.

## Contacts

Questions about this policy should be raised with the HR Business Partner for
your department or through a PeopleHub ticket in the *Leave & Attendance*
category.
